FEA of Wind Turbine Lattice Tower

Performed full Finite Element Analysis on a 3D wind turbine lattice tower using a custom MATLAB FEA solver. Modeled 4 critical loading cases — vertical and horizontal centrifugal forces in ±Z and ±Y directions — computing nodal displacements, support reactions, and member stresses across 250+ truss elements.
